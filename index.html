📍 Create VistaDigital AI GBP

Step-by-step — takes about 20 minutes

  • 1
    Go to Google Business Profile Manager
    Open while logged into the Google account you want tied to VistaDigital AI.
    → business.google.com
  • 2
    Click "Add your business to Google"
    Type: VistaDigital AI. If Google shows a match, it's a different business — create new anyway.
  • 3
    Choose the right category
    Primary: Marketing Agency
    Secondary: Website Designer · Advertising Agency
    Do NOT pick Computer Repair or anything tech-generic.
  • 4
    Choose "Service area business" — not a storefront
    You go to clients. Don't enter a home address. Service area: Tulsa, Broken Arrow, Bixby, Jenks, Owasso + United States for digital.
  • 5
    Add contact info
    Phone: your business number · Website: https://vistadigitalai.com · Email: grow@vistadigitalai.com
  • 6
    Verify — phone is fastest
    Phone verification = instant code. If not available: video verification (30-sec clip at your desk showing business name). Postcard = 5–14 days, avoid.
  • 7
    Paste this description after verification
    VistaDigital AI is a Tulsa-based digital marketing agency helping small businesses generate more leads online. We specialize in website design, local SEO, Google Ads, Meta advertising, GoHighLevel CRM setup, and AI marketing automation. Founded in 2025. We serve contractors, home service companies, and local businesses in Tulsa and nationwide for digital services.
  • 8
    Add services + photos
    Services: Website Design · Local SEO · Google Ads · Meta Ads · GoHighLevel CRM · AI Automation · Social Media

    Photos: your logo · screenshot of a client result (blur if needed) · your headshot as founder.
