📍 Create VistaDigital AI GBP

Step-by-step — takes about 20 minutes

  • 1
    Go to Google Business Profile Manager
    Open this in your browser while logged into your Google account (the one you want tied to the business).
    → business.google.com
  • 2
    Click "Add your business to Google"
    Type the business name: VistaDigital AI. If Google shows a match, it's a different business — create new anyway.
  • 3
    Choose the right category
    Primary category: "Marketing Agency"
    Also add secondary: "Website Designer" and "Advertising Agency"
    Do NOT pick "Computer Repair" or anything tech-generic.
  • 4
    Choose "Service area business" (not storefront)
    You go to clients — do not enter a physical address if you work from home. Enter your service area: Tulsa, Broken Arrow, Bixby, Jenks, Owasso + "United States" for digital services.
  • 5
    Add contact info
    Phone: your business number
    Website: https://vistadigitalai.com
    Email: grow@vistadigitalai.com
  • 6
    Verify the listing
    Choose phone verification (fastest — instant code). If phone option isn't available, choose video verification (record a 30-sec clip showing you working at your desk with a document showing the business name). Postcard takes 5–14 days — avoid if possible.
  • 7
    Complete the profile after verification
    Paste this description:
    VistaDigital AI is a Tulsa-based digital marketing agency helping small businesses generate more leads online. We specialize in website design, local SEO, Google Ads, Meta advertising, GoHighLevel CRM setup, and AI marketing automation. Founded in 2025. We serve contractors, home service companies, and local businesses in Tulsa and nationwide for digital services.
  • 8
    Add services and photos
    Services to add: Website Design · Local SEO · Google Ads Management · Meta Ads Management · GoHighLevel CRM Setup · AI Automation · Social Media Management

    Photos: your logo, a screenshot of a client result (blurred if needed), your profile photo as founder.
